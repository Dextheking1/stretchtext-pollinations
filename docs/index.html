Stretchtext

Turn any article into stretchtext. Click a sentence and it grows in place.

Stretchtext is an idea from 1967 (Ted Nelson): text that grows where the reader wants more and shrinks back where they don't. This browser extension brings it to any web page. Sentences that can stretch get a dotted underline; click one and a fuller version replaces it, right where it stands. Click more to go deeper, less to step back.

Expansions are generated with Pollinations and paid for with your own Pollen. The extension has no server and no key of its own.

Install

No store listing yet, so load it unpacked. It takes a minute:

  1. Download the source from the GitHub repository (green Code button, Download ZIP) and unzip it.
  2. Open chrome://extensions in Chrome or Edge, or about:debugging in Firefox.
  3. Turn on Developer mode (Chrome/Edge).
  4. Click Load unpacked and pick the unzipped folder (the one with manifest.json in it). Firefox: Load Temporary Add-on, pick manifest.json.
  5. Pin the Stretchtext button to your toolbar.

Connect your Pollen

  1. Open the extension's Options page.
  2. Click Connect with Pollinations and approve on the Pollinations page that opens.
  3. The approved key stays in your browser only. It has a budget and an expiry you control, and you can revoke it from your Pollinations dashboard at any time.

Use it

Open any article, click the Stretchtext toolbar button (or press Alt+Shift+S), choose Enable on this page, then click a dotted sentence. Turning Stretchtext off restores the page exactly as it was.

Privacy

No tracking, no analytics. The extension only talks to gen.pollinations.ai (the sentence you clicked, its paragraph, your key) and enter.pollinations.ai (sign-in only).
